Kinertia achievements

XP and achievements for a team-built productivity app.

Internship · 2025–26 · Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL

Private company repo: no public code or demo. My work: PRs #39 and #45, both merged.

Problem

Completing a task or goal awarded no XP, and there were no achievements.

What I did

PR #39 awards XP on completion. PR #45 adds the achievement service, a rule evaluator, and a 50-item catalog.

Result

Merged January 2026. Goals award 50–500 XP; tasks 8–25 XP. Achievements are checked after every award.

How it works

Key decision

Rewards must never fail a completion.

Why
Finishing the task matters more than the reward, so both reward calls are isolated.
Trade-off
A failed award only shows up in logs, and nothing retries it.
