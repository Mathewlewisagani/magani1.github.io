Internet Quality Thresholds

How much packet delay does it take to stall a live stream?

Research · 2026 · C, Linux NFQUEUE, Python, Docker, NetGent, Snakemake

Paper (PDF)My NetGent commits ↗Research summary →

No live demo: the testbed needs three machines.

Problem

Internet quality scores need a threshold for when latency makes an app unusable. Today those thresholds come from expert opinion.

What I did

First author, with Prof. Ranysha Ware. I built the testbed, wrote the C program that delays packets, and automated the sweeps and analysis. In NetGent I wrote the YouTube Live and Twitch workflows.

Result

No added delay: 0% rebuffering. 25 ms on one packet in five: 31.9% median rebuffering. Accepted as a poster paper at ACM CoNEXT 2026.

Testbed

Key decision

Pin the stream to 1080p and sample the player every 0.1 s.

Why
With resolution fixed, delay can only show up as buffering. Sampling every 0.1 s catches stalls under a second.
Trade-off
It hides how adaptive bitrate would trade quality for smoothness.
